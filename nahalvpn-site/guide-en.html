Install on Android

  1. On the download page, tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installing from this source for your browser or file manager, then go back.
  3. Tap Install, then open the app.

The app is in Persian by default. To switch to English, open تنظیمات (Settings) and change زبان (Language). If the normal file does not install, take the armv7 (older phones) or universal file from "all files" on the download page.

Install on Windows

  1. On the download page, click Download for Windows. You need 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.
  2. Open the file. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. When setup finishes, open NahalVPN from the Start menu.

On Windows, browsers and most apps go through the VPN. A few apps, such as some games, ignore the Windows proxy setting and do not go through it.

Sign in

Get your username and password from your seller and enter them on the sign-in screen. Each account works on one device by default.

If the app says the account is active on another device, pick that device in the list and tap Remove and sign in. The other device is signed out.

Connect

  1. Choose a location (for example Germany).
  2. Tap the large Connect button.
  3. The first time, Android asks for permission to set up a VPN. Tap OK.

Once connected, the top of the screen says "Connected". Ping shows the response time and Speed test shows download and upload speed. Both run only when you tap them.

To disconnect, tap the same button, which now says Disconnect.

Choose a protocol

On the home screen, pick one from the Protocol list:

  • Automatic (recommended): tries VLESS first and switches to OpenVPN by itself if your network blocks it.
  • VLESS: the fastest; the traffic looks like normal web browsing.
  • OpenVPN (UDP): a classic VPN that works on most networks.
  • OpenVPN (TCP): slower, but gets through strict networks.

If you pick a protocol yourself, the app connects only with that one and does not switch to another.

Kill switch

With the Kill switch on, if the connection drops, the internet stays blocked until NahalVPN reconnects, so nothing leaks outside the VPN. Turn it on or off in Settings.

On Android, for full protection also turn on "Always-on VPN" and "Block connections without VPN" for NahalVPN in the phone's VPN settings. The Open VPN settings button in NahalVPN's settings takes you there.

Devices and expiry

Your subscription's end date is on the home screen and under Account. There is no data limit.

Under Account you see the devices signed in to your account. Remove signs a device out and frees its place for another one. To renew or get more devices, contact your seller.

If it does not connect

"Could not connect on this network"
Check that your internet works without the VPN. Set the protocol to Automatic or try OpenVPN (TCP). On Wi-Fi, try once with mobile data.
"Could not connect with … on this network"
The protocol you chose is blocked on this network. Choose another protocol or Automatic.
"Wrong username or password"
Check upper and lower case and do not add spaces. If you forgot the password, your seller can make a new one.
"Too many attempts"
After several wrong passwords, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask your seller for a new password.
"Your subscription has expired" or "This account is disabled"
Contact your seller to renew or re-enable it. After renewing, just tap Connect again.
"You were signed out on this device"
This device was removed from your account elsewhere. Sign in again.
"No connection to NahalVPN"
Your phone or computer is offline or the connection is very weak. Check it and try again. If another VPN is on, turn it off.
Android: the connection drops after a while
Turn off battery optimization for NahalVPN in the phone's battery settings so Android does not close it.
Windows: no internet after the app closed unexpectedly
Open NahalVPN again; the Windows proxy setting is put back automatically. If NahalVPN is open and the kill switch is on, being offline until it reconnects is on purpose; click Disconnect to get normal internet back.
To renew, get a new password, or for anything this guide does not solve, contact your seller.
